# Corrections to the original texts

Every change made to the texts you supplied (the intro and your seven outside scenarios), so you can check each one before recording. All new texts (dust, the nine new outside scenarios, the inside scenarios, short answers and exam) were written fresh in the same style and are not listed here.

## Rules applied everywhere

- **Verbs after «أَنْ» take nasb.** The Must lists are completions of «تَذَكَّرْ أَنْ ...», so each verb ends in fatha: أَتَوَقَّفُ → أَتَوَقَّفَ، أَمْشِي → أَمْشِيَ، أُنَادِي → أُنَادِيَ. Fixed in scenarios 2, 4, 6 and 7.
- **Remember lists stay in rafʿ** after «لَا» (negation, not prohibition): لَا أَرْكُضُ.
- **Tanween fath is written on the letter before the alif** in one style: يَسَارًا، فَوْرًا، جَيِّدًا (never يَسَارَاً).
- **Every text has a boy and a girl version**, written by hand with `{boy/girl}` markup, including the slogans.

## Changes to meaning or order

- **Scenario 3 (reaching school):** the steps were reordered so the student stops, looks, waits, then crosses, in the real order. «التَّأَكُّدِ مِنْ خُلُوِّ الطَّرِيقِ» and «حَتَّى يَخْلُوَ الطَّرِيقُ» replace the unclear wording about the road being empty.
- **Scenario 4 (crossing with friends):** the opening spoke only to the boys in the scene. A vocative for the student was added («وَأَنْ{تَ/تِ} يَا {بَطَلُ/بَطَلَةُ}») so the answer also addresses whoever asked. It had no slogan, so one was added: «{قِفُوا/قِفْنَ}.. {انْظُرُوا/انْظُرْنَ}.. {تَمَاسَكُوا/تَمَاسَكْنَ} بِالْأَيْدِي.. {وَاعْبُرُوا/وَاعْبُرْنَ} مَعًا بِأَمَانٍ!»
- **Scenario 7 (heavy rain):** two overlapping Must items were merged so the list stays at six. The opening now ends with «فَتَذَكَّرْ» like the others, and a new slogan was written to match the rain steps.

| Was | Now | Why |
