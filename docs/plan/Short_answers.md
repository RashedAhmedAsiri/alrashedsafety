# Short answers mode

## How short answers work

A short answer is for a quick, specific question that does not need a full scenario. When one matches, Alrashed walks (or the camera cuts) to the listed place, faces the camera and speaks the answer with the listed expression. There is no before or after animation, only his face and small gestures. Each answer has two pre-recorded audio files (boy and girl) with viseme JSON, like the scenarios.

- **Matching:** the same keyword plus meaning score as scenarios. If a short answer and a scenario both pass, the one with the higher score wins; on a tie (within 0.05) the short answer wins in Short answer mode and the scenario wins in every other mode.
- **Asks** lists sample student phrasings in Saudi dialect for the matcher's training set. Add more as testing finds new ones.
- **Answer** is the exact text to record, written in `{boy/girl}` markup.
- **Place** is the location ID; **Face** is the expression held while speaking (talking visemes run on top of it).
