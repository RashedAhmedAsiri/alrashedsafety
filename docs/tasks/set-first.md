camera/robot fields of `computer_lab`, `biology_lab`, `art_room`, `library` in `src/core/locations.ts`.

Rooms (first floor; see the "Inside the school" table in `docs/plan/3D_world.md`):
- `computer_lab`: 16 computers on desks, chairs on wheels, cable trays along the walls, power strips with
  safety covers, a wall socket at child height, a projector and screen, a printer, a «ممنوع الأكل
  والشرب» picture sign, an AC unit.
- `biology_lab`: lab benches with sinks, stools, a skeleton model, plant pots under a grow light, a
  microscope on each bench, beakers and test tubes in racks, colored liquids in labeled bottles behind a
  glass cabinet, goggles and gloves on hooks, an eyewash station, a broken-glass bin, a fish tank.
- `art_room`: big shared tables, paint pots, brushes in jars, round-tip scissors in a tray, glue,
  colored paper, a drying rack with paintings, easels, a sink.
- `library`: low bookshelves with colorful books, a reading carpet, bean bags, a librarian desk, a globe.
